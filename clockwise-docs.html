1. Executive Summary & Product Overview

Clockwise is a dedicated employee attendance tracking web application built to automate daily shift recording, eliminate paper timesheets, and enforce accurate attendance compliance with real-time status reporting.

Employees authenticate securely, click to begin their workday, click when their shift ends, and Clockwise automatically calculates exact shift durations, net worked minutes, and policy compliance (Present, Late, Incomplete, Absent) without manual data entry.

Key Features:

  • One-Click Shift Clocking: Intuitive 3-state clock card: Not Clocked In → Working (with real-time counter) → Shift Completed.
  • Automated Compliance Engine: Automatically classifies each shift into Present, Late, Incomplete, or Absent based on schedule criteria.
  • Weekend Protection: Saturdays and Sundays are permanently designated as rest days and are never penalized as absences.
  • Dual Attendance Views: Monthly calendar with color-coded status badges plus a detailed chronological tabular audit log.
  • SELISE Blocks IAM Security: Hosted OIDC login, token exchange, email-based user invitations, account activation, and password resets.
  • Native Multilingual Localization: Live client translations in English, German, and Bengali, including locale-specific date and time formatting.

2. Authentication & Account Management (Blocks IAM)

Clockwise integrates directly with the SELISE Blocks Identity & Access Management (IAM) infrastructure. No unencrypted credentials or sensitive authentication secrets are stored on the client.

2.1 Sign-In Flow

  1. Navigate to the Clockwise login screen (/login).
  2. Select preferred language if desired (English, Deutsch, বাংলা) in the top-right corner.
  3. Click "Sign In with Blocks".
  4. The user is redirected to the secure, hosted SELISE Blocks IAM portal.
  5. Enter registered corporate email and password.
  6. Upon authentication, Blocks sets a secure httpOnly cookie and redirects back to /login/callback.
  7. Clockwise validates the session token and navigates directly to the employee Dashboard.

2.2 New Employee Invitation & Activation

When an administrator provisions a new employee record:

  1. Blocks dispatches an automated invitation email to the employee's corporate address.
  2. The email contains a one-time activation link: /oidc/activate/?code=<ACTIVATION_CODE>.
  3. Clicking the link navigates to the Clockwise Activate Account interface.
  4. The employee specifies and confirms a secure password (minimum 8 characters).
  5. Clicking "Set Password & Activate" finalizes account provisioning on Blocks IAM.
  6. The employee is immediately eligible to sign in.

2.3 Password Recovery ("Forgot Password")

  1. On the Clockwise login screen (/login), click "Forgot your password?".
  2. Provide the registered email address and click "Send Reset Link".
  3. Blocks dispatches a password recovery email containing a unique reset token.
  4. The user opens the link pointing to /resetpassword?code=<RESET_CODE>.
  5. The user provides and confirms the new password and clicks "Save New Password".

3. Attendance Rules & Status Determination Engine

Clockwise eliminates administrative ambiguity by evaluating attendance rules through a deterministic calculation engine.

3.1 Standard Schedule Policy

  • Standard Working Days: Monday through Friday.
  • Rest Days (Weekends): Saturday and Sunday (never penalized).
  • Official Shift Window: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (9 total elapsed hours including 1 hour break = 8 required working hours / 480 minutes).

3.2 Status Determination Matrix

Attendance Status Badge Condition Rule Details
Present Present On-Time & Full Shift Clocked in on or before 9:15 AM AND total worked duration is ≥ 8 hours (480 mins).
Late Late Late Clock-In Clocked in at 9:16 AM or later, regardless of total shift duration.
Incomplete Incomplete Short Duration / No Out Clocked in before 9:15 AM, but either forgot to clock out OR worked less than 8 hours total.
Absent Absent No Attendance A standard business weekday (Mon–Fri) concluded with zero clock-in records.
Weekend Weekend Scheduled Rest Days Saturdays and Sundays. Automatically excluded from working day totals and absence metrics.
Official Grace Period Policy
The standard workday begins at 9:00 AM. Clockwise includes an official 15-minute grace window. A clock-in recorded between 9:00 AM and 9:15 AM is considered strictly on-time. Any clock-in at 9:16 AM or later is automatically flagged as Late.

4. User Interface & Feature Walkthrough

4.1 Dashboard (/dashboard)

The operational cockpit for daily attendance management:

  • Contextual Greeting & Clock: Real-time greeting with employee name, live digital clock, and localized date.
  • Interactive Clock Card:
    • State 1 (Not Clocked In): Displays current time and the prominent primary "CLOCK IN" button.
    • State 2 (Working): Displays start time, live animated pulse badge, active elapsed duration counter, and the red "CLOCK OUT" button.
    • State 3 (Shift Complete): Confirmation card displaying clock-in time, clock-out time, and total calculated duration.
  • Monthly Metric Cards: Real-time counts of Present Days, Absent Days, Late Days, and overall Attendance Rate %.

4.2 Attendance History (/attendance)

  • Month Navigator: Move smoothly between past, current, and future calendar months.
  • Interactive Calendar Grid: Monthly calendar displaying weekday columns, weekend indicators, and color-coded status badges on worked dates. Clicking any date card displays a detailed summary card.
  • Chronological Data Table: Formatted table listing Date, Clock In, Clock Out, Total Working Duration, and Status badges.

4.3 Employee Profile (/profile)

Displays verified account identity details:

  • Employee Full Name & Corporate Email.
  • Employee ID & Departmental Assignment.
  • Secure "Sign Out" button to invalidate session credentials and clear local state.

6. Chatbot Knowledge Base & Q&A Retrieval Bank

The following 17 structured Q&A pairs are designed for direct ingestion into RAG knowledge bases, LLM system prompts, or customer support chatbot vector stores:

Q01 What is Clockwise?
Clockwise is a modern web application designed for employee attendance tracking. It enables staff to clock in when starting work and clock out when finishing, while automatically calculating attendance compliance, working durations, and monthly summaries.
Q02 How do I sign in to Clockwise?
Navigate to the Clockwise login page and click "Sign In with Blocks". You will be redirected to the secure SELISE Blocks IAM portal. Enter your corporate email and password. Once verified, you will be redirected back to your Clockwise dashboard.
Q03 How do I clock in at the start of my shift?
Log in to Clockwise and go to your Dashboard. In the "Today's Attendance" card, click the blue "CLOCK IN" button. The system immediately captures your timestamp and starts tracking your active shift duration.
Q04 How do I clock out at the end of my day?
Navigate to your Dashboard where your Clock Card indicates you are currently working. Click the red "CLOCK OUT" button. Clockwise records your end timestamp, calculates your total shift duration, and finalizes your attendance status for the day.
Q05 What time do I need to clock in to be marked "Present"?
The standard workday starts at 9:00 AM. Clockwise includes a 15-minute grace period, meaning you must clock in on or before 9:15 AM AND complete at least 8 hours (480 minutes) of working duration to be classified as "Present".
Q06 What happens if I clock in after 9:15 AM?
Any clock-in recorded at 9:16 AM or later is automatically flagged as "Late", regardless of how many total hours you work during that day.
Q07 Why does my attendance status display "Incomplete"?
A shift status displays "Incomplete" if you clocked in but failed to clock out, or if you clocked out with less than 8 hours total duration (provided you clocked in before 9:15 AM).
Q08 Are Saturdays and Sundays counted as absences?
No. Saturdays and Sundays are recognized non-working weekend days. They are never counted as working business days and are never marked as absences.
Q09 How is the Attendance Rate percentage calculated?
The Attendance Rate is calculated as: (Total Present Days / Total Elapsed Business Working Days in the Month) × 100. Weekends and future dates are excluded from the denominator.
Q10 How do I view my previous attendance records?
Click "Attendance" in the navigation sidebar. You can inspect your records through an interactive monthly calendar with color-coded status badges, or view a chronological table showing date, clock in, clock out, and total duration.
Q11 How do I activate a new Clockwise account?
When an administrator invites you, Blocks sends an activation email with a secure link. Click the link to open the Clockwise Account Activation page, set your password (at least 8 characters), and click "Set Password & Activate". You can then log in immediately.
Q12 What should I do if I forgot my password?
On the login screen, click "Forgot your password?". Enter your registered email address and click "Send Reset Link". Open the link sent to your inbox, choose a new password, and click "Save New Password".
Q13 Can I use Clockwise in German or Bengali?
Yes. Clockwise is fully localized in English, German (Deutsch), and Bengali (বাংলা). Switch languages anytime using the selector on the login screen, sidebar, or mobile menu.
Q14 Can I clock in multiple times in a single day?
Clockwise records one complete shift per day (clock in when your day starts, clock out when your day concludes). Once you have clocked out, the daily shift is complete.
Q15 How do I log out of Clockwise?
Click the "Log Out" button at the bottom of the navigation sidebar, or navigate to your Profile page (/profile) and click the red "Log Out" button to end your session.
Q16 What do the calendar status colors mean?
Green: Present (Clocked in ≤ 9:15 AM and worked ≥ 8 hours)
Amber: Late (Clocked in after 9:15 AM)
Red: Absent (Business day with no attendance recorded)
Blue: Incomplete (Missing clock-out or under 8 hours worked)
Gray: Weekend rest day
Q17 Can I access Clockwise on my mobile phone?
Yes. Clockwise features a fully responsive mobile layout. You can access all clocking functions, view calendar summaries, and manage your profile from any modern smartphone browser.
